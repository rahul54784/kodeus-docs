![Onboarding risk preferences](/docs/images/warren/onboarding-risk.png)

After authentication, Warren begins a guided onboarding flow to personalize strategy recommendations.

The onboarding experience currently collects:

- Trading experience
- Risk preferences
- Trading style preferences
- Preferred market types

## Trading experience

You currently select one of the following:

- Beginner
- Novice
- Experienced
- Seasoned

This information helps Warren:

- Personalize recommendations
- Tailor strategy discovery
- Adjust onboarding guidance
- Optimize portfolio suggestions

## Risk profile

You configure a preferred portfolio behavior. Available profiles:

- Conservative
- Balanced
- Aggressive

Each profile adjusts:

- Position sizing behavior
- Portfolio volatility exposure
- Strategy selection tendencies
- Allocation behavior

You can modify these preferences later through settings.

## Trading style

![Onboarding strategies](/docs/images/warren/onboarding-strategies.png)

You can select preferred trading styles. Current options include:

- Scalping
- Day Trading
- Swing Trading
- Position Trading

These selections help Warren prioritize compatible strategies.

## Market type

![Onboarding volume](/docs/images/warren/onboarding-volume.png)

You currently choose preferred market environments. Supported options:

- Spot
- Futures

This preference helps Warren tailor strategy recommendations and execution environments.
