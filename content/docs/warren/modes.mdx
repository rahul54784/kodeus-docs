After selecting strategies, you choose how you want to interact with Warren. Current trading modes:

- Live Trading
- Paper Trading
- Demo Environment

## Paper trading

Paper trading lets you:

- Simulate execution
- Evaluate strategies
- Monitor performance
- Explore Warren without risking real capital

Paper trading currently uses:

- Live market data
- Simulated capital
- Runtime monitoring
- Real-time strategy behavior

No real funds are used during paper trading.

## Live trading

Live trading allows Warren to execute strategies using your connected exchanges. Live trading requires:

- Exchange connection
- API authorization
- Capital allocation
- Strategy activation

You remain fully in control of:

- Exchange permissions
- Allocation boundaries
- Execution limits
- Strategy runtime behavior

## Demo environment

The demo environment lets you:

- Explore the platform
- Browse strategies
- Understand workflows
- Navigate Warren without connecting an exchange

Demo is the friction-free path if you want to look around before authenticating an exchange.
