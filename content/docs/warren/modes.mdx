After selecting strategies, you choose how you want to interact with Warren. Current trading modes:

- Live Trading
- Paper Trading
- Demo Environment

<Callout type="info">
**Paper is always the default, and it needs no exchange account.** Every execution path in Warren, both strategy bots and Warren's own book, runs in paper mode first: simulated capital against live market data, zero risk. Going live is never automatic. You connect an exchange with your own keys and explicitly switch a specific bot or position to live, only when you are ready.
</Callout>

## Paper trading

Paper trading lets you:

- Simulate execution
- Evaluate strategies
- Monitor performance
- Explore Warren without risking real capital

Paper trading currently uses:

- Live market data
- Simulated capital
- Runtime monitoring
- Real-time strategy behavior

No real funds are used during paper trading, and no API keys are needed to start. This is the honest bridge between a backtest and live capital: the same strategy runs against the same live market you would trade, without a cent at stake.

## Live trading

Live trading allows Warren to execute strategies using your connected exchanges. Live trading requires:

- Exchange connection
- API authorization
- Capital allocation
- Strategy activation

You remain fully in control of:

- Exchange permissions
- Allocation boundaries
- Execution limits
- Strategy runtime behavior

<Callout type="warn">
Live mode moves real funds, so every entry and exit passes a mandatory pre-trade risk review, the leverage cap, and a confirm-to-approve step: Warren proposes, you approve. Nothing trades live by accident.
</Callout>

## Demo environment

The demo environment lets you:

- Explore the platform
- Browse strategies
- Understand workflows
- Navigate Warren without connecting an exchange

Demo is the friction-free path if you want to look around before authenticating an exchange.

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Deep dive: paper vs. live, under the hood">

The two modes are not cosmetic. In paper mode, strategy bots trade a simulated budget and Warren's discretionary book runs against a simulated sandbox, orders fill against the live price without ever reaching a venue. In live mode, the same actions route to your connected exchange through your own encrypted API keys.

Everything else, the risk gate, the leverage cap, the confirm-to-approve step, behaves identically in both modes, so what you practice in paper is exactly what you get live.

</Accordion>
