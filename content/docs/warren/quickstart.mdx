You can experience the entire Warren workflow, a market read, a validated strategy, and a running bot, without risking a cent. Everything below happens in **paper mode**, and each step is simply a message you send Warren in plain English.

<Callout type="info">Paper mode uses simulated funds and needs no exchange account. Going live is a separate, deliberate step covered in [Trading Modes](/docs/warren/modes).</Callout>

## Your first ten minutes

<Steps>
<Step>
**Sign in.** Open Warren and authenticate with your wallet or email, then complete the short [onboarding](/docs/warren/onboarding) so recommendations fit your risk profile and trading style. On first entry you also choose how your capital is split across three buckets, adjustable at any time; see [how your capital is organized](/docs/warren/onboarding#how-your-capital-is-organized).
</Step>
<Step>
**Ask for a market read.** Try *"What's the market doing for BTC?"* Note the regime and the verdict that travels with it. If Warren abstains, ask why: the reasons are always concrete.
</Step>
<Step>
**Find a fitting strategy.** Ask *"Find me a strategy that suits this market."* Warren ranks candidates by fit to the current regime and can explain any strategy's rules in plain language.
</Step>
<Step>
**Validate it honestly.** Ask *"Backtest it across bull, bear, and ranging windows."* Read the worst regime, not the average, then follow up with a full-period run to see what running it blindly would feel like.
</Step>
<Step>
**Deploy on paper.** Say *"Paper-trade it with a 5% profit target and 5% max loss."* Review the confirmation card, which includes a pre-trade risk check, and approve.
</Step>
<Step>
**Come back tomorrow.** Ask *"How are my bots doing?"* to see P&L, which guards fired, and the full trade history. Stop anytime with *"Stop it"* or *"Stop all my bots."*
</Step>
</Steps>

![A live strategy running on paper](/docs/images/warren/strategy-live.png)

<Callout type="warn">Every money action, including deploying a bot, needs your explicit confirmation. Warren proposes the exact action; you approve it.</Callout>

## Paper first, live when ready

Everything starts in paper mode: strategy bots trade simulated funds and Warren's own book runs against a simulated sandbox, with no exchange account required. Going live is an explicit step: connect an exchange with your own API keys, on any of the [seven supported venues](/docs/warren/exchanges), and switch the specific bot or position to live, as covered in [Trading Modes](/docs/warren/modes). Optionally connect Telegram to receive [signals and alerts](/docs/warren/signals) off-platform.
