![Portfolio](/docs/images/warren/portfolio.png)

Before launching execution, Warren requires you to configure capital allocation.

## What you configure

- Connected exchange
- Trading pair
- Allocation amount
- Execution boundaries

## Supported flows

- Allocation presets
- Custom allocation values
- Exchange-specific configuration
- Trading pair selection

Capital allocation is the final guardrail you set before a strategy goes live. Warren operates strictly within the amount, pair, and execution boundaries you define here.
